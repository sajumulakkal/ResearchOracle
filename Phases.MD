
| Phase | Name | Objective | Major Deliverables | Business Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 0** | Baseline PoC | Demonstrate research-to-portfolio workflow | MockResearchOracleFeed, ResearchSignalTrigger, MockPortfolioManager, BUY/HOLD/REDUCE signals | Proof of concept and architecture validation |
| **Phase 1** | Trusted Research Oracle | Replace single-owner oracle with verifiable oracle network | Multi-node oracle network, 2-of-3 signatures, research hash storage, versioning, timestamp validation, research commitments | Institutional trust and auditability |
| **Phase 2** | Enhanced Research Intelligence | Expand research factors beyond valuation and quality score | CFROI, Business Resilience, Accounting Quality, Risk Score, Momentum Score, ESG metrics | More sophisticated investment decisions |
| **Phase 3** | Multi-Asset Portfolio Support | Move from single-stock to multi-asset portfolio | Multiple tickers, portfolio allocation engine, position tracking, portfolio-level decisions, portfolio composite scoring, rebalance instruction generation, target weight management | Diversified portfolio management |
| **Phase 3.5** | Integrated Research Intelligence Platform | Consolidate and operationalize capabilities developed in Phases 0-3 into a unified platform | End-to-end research-to-portfolio workflow, integrated oracle network, enhanced research scoring, multi-asset portfolio engine, portfolio monitoring, unified APIs and data model | Establishes a production-ready foundation for tokenization, governance, risk management, and AI automation |
| **Phase 4** | Tokenized Fund Layer | Create investable fund structure on-chain | SVF Token, fund ownership representation, subscriptions, redemptions | Tokenized investment product |
| **Phase 5** | NAV Oracle & Fund Accounting | Provide fund valuation transparency | NAV calculations, fund valuation oracle, portfolio valuation engine, investor reporting | Operationally viable tokenized fund |
| **Phase 6** | Governance & Controls | Introduce governance and approval workflows | Investment committee voting, rule approval workflow, change management, on-chain governance | Institutional controls and oversight |
| **Phase 7** | Chainlink & External Data Integration | Replace mock components with production-grade integrations | Chainlink Automation, market data feeds, external pricing, corporate action feeds | Production-ready oracle infrastructure |
| **Phase 8** | Risk Management Framework | Introduce institutional risk controls | Position limits, sector limits, concentration controls, drawdown monitoring, compliance checks | Risk-aware portfolio management |
| **Phase 9** | Smart Equity Lending Platform (SELP) | Extend portfolio assets into lending ecosystem | Collateral management, dynamic LTV, research-based collateral scoring, automated monitoring | New revenue opportunities and lending use cases |
| **Phase 10** | AI Research Intelligence Platform | Automate conversion of research into structured signals | AI extraction layer, research summarization, signal generation, research classification, research scoring | Fully automated research intelligence ecosystem |
